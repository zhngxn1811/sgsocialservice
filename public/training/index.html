Training directory

Find training for the way you want to practise

Courses for social workers, counsellors and psychologists in Singapore, from foundation skills to specialist approaches like CBT, motivational interviewing, play therapy, trauma-informed care and supervision. Filter them yourself, or tell the training assistant what you'd like to learn and get a learning plan picked for you. Every course links to the provider's own page, where you register.

Fees, accreditation and grants are shown as each provider publishes them. They change often, and what you pay depends on your age, citizenship and employer, so always confirm on the provider's page before you register.

AI training assistant

Tell us what you'd like to learn

Share your role or studies, how long you've been practising, what you want to get better at, and any limits like budget, who's paying or online only. The assistant suggests courses in a sensible order, with fees, funding you may qualify for, and accreditation.

Leave out contact and ID details. No NRIC, phone numbers, emails or addresses. Your role, experience and goals are enough. Nothing you type is stored.

You can reply to refine the plan, for example "only funded courses" or "what counts towards SAC CPD?"
