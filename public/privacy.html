Privacy

Last updated November 2026

What this site is

SG Social Services is an independent, volunteer-run directory for counsellors and social workers in Singapore. It isn't affiliated with MSF or any listed agency.

Case summaries you type into the assistant

Only enter de-identified information. Don't include names, NRIC or FIN numbers, addresses, phone numbers, school names or anything else that could identify a person. The site blocks some obvious identifiers, but it can't catch everything, so the responsibility stays with you and your agency's data protection policies.

When you press "Find relevant services", your case summary is sent to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI model, to generate suggestions. This site does not save case summaries in any database or log. Anthropic's handling of that data is set out in its own commercial terms and privacy policy.

Recommendations and update reports

When you use the form, we store what you type (service details, your description and your role if you give it), along with the date. We use it only to review and update the directory. We don't collect your name or email through the form.

The mobile app

The SG Social Services app for Android and iOS works the same way as this website. Services you save with the star, and your light or dark appearance choice, are kept only on your phone and are never sent to us. The app keeps a copy of the directory on your phone so it works offline. It doesn't use accounts, location, contacts, advertising or tracking.

The jobs board and jobs assistant

The board lists jobs from public sources and from employers. We store employer submissions for review and publish approved job descriptions, qualifications and application links, including any recruitment email address supplied for that purpose. Jobseekers apply directly to the employer or through another job portal. We don't collect job applications, CVs or jobseekers' contact details, and we don't share anything about jobseekers with employers.

What you type into the jobs assistant is sent to Anthropic's Claude model to pick out suitable jobs and write your recommendations. It isn't saved on our side: the conversation lives in your browser tab and is gone when you press "Start over" or close the page. Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models. Please leave out your NRIC, phone number, email and address; the assistant refuses messages that contain them. We only count how many times the jobs assistant was used each day.

Visit counts

To show funders and partners that the directory is used, the site keeps simple daily counts: how many visits and page views there were, how many searches were made, which presenting-issue pages and filters were used, and how many times the assistant was used. That's all. We don't use cookies for this, and we don't store your IP address, device details, the words you search for or anything you type into the assistant. If your browser has Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control switched on, you aren't counted at all.

We also count roughly how many different people visit, and which country visits come from. For the first, your browser remembers the last date it was counted (for example "19 November 2026") so it isn't counted twice in a day or month; that date stays on your device and only "+1 visitor" is sent to us. For the second, our host, Netlify, works out the country from your connection and we add 1 to that country's count. We never see or store your IP address or anything more precise than the country.

The optional question about you

The first time you scroll to the directory or use the assistant, we may ask two optional questions: your role (for example counsellor or social worker) and the type of place you work (for example a Family Service Centre or a hospital). We use broad categories only and never ask for your name, email or organisation's name. Your answer is added to anonymous totals, which help us show funders who the directory serves. If you've told us your role, we also count assistant uses by role, as totals only.

So we don't ask again, your browser remembers that you answered or chose "Not now". This stays on your device and is never sent to us. To be asked again, clear this site's data in your browser. People with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control switched on are never asked.

Other data

Your light or dark appearance preference is saved in this browser so it can be used on your next visit.

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and to prevent abuse. We don't use advertising trackers.

Not professional advice

The assistant suggests services from this directory. It isn't clinical, legal or financial advice. Always confirm details with the service and use your professional judgement and your agency's protocols, especially where there is risk.
