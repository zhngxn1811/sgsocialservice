Privacy

Last updated November 2026

What this site is

SG Social Services is an independent, volunteer-run directory for counsellors and social workers in Singapore. It isn't affiliated with MSF or any listed agency.

Case summaries you type into the assistant

Only enter de-identified information. Don't include names, NRIC or FIN numbers, addresses, phone numbers, school names or anything else that could identify a person. The site blocks some obvious identifiers, but it can't catch everything, so the responsibility stays with you and your agency's data protection policies.

When you press "Find relevant services", your case summary is sent to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI model, to generate suggestions. This site does not save case summaries in any database or log. Anthropic's handling of that data is set out in its own commercial terms and privacy policy.

Recommendations and update reports

When you use the form, we store what you type (service details, your description and your role if you give it), along with the date. We use it only to review and update the directory. We don't collect your name or email through the form.

Other data

Your light or dark appearance preference is saved in this browser so it can be used on your next visit.

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and to prevent abuse. We don't use advertising trackers.

Not professional advice

The assistant suggests services from this directory. It isn't clinical, legal or financial advice. Always confirm details with the service and use your professional judgement and your agency's protocols, especially where there is risk.
