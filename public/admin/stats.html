Site numbers

Visits, searches, assistant use and submissions, for reports and funding applications. Counts only: no cookies, and nothing that identifies a person is stored.

Visits per day

A visit is someone opening the site, counted once per browser session. Hover or tap a day for more.

Interest by presenting issue

Views of each issue page plus uses of its filter on the home page. Useful for showing where demand is.

Daily numbers table

People who switch on Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control in their browser aren't counted, and search engines are filtered out, so real use is a little higher than shown. Assistant uses are counted on the server each time a case is sent; the case text is never stored.
